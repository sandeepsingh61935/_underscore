Flexible box layout

CSS Flexible Box Layout is a layout model for arranging items in rows or columns. Items flex to fill extra space or shrink to fit.

The main idea behind the flex layout is to give the container the ability to alter its items' width and height to best fill the available space.

A flex container expands items to fill available free space or shrinks them to prevent overflow.
